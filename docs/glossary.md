# Glossary — Shared Vocabulary

Terms used across the project with a fixed meaning, so the three minds and any
later reader share one language.

- **Persona / Mind / Seat** — one of the three interpretive lenses (Herald,
  Heretic, Cartographer). Model-agnostic; defined by lens, not vendor.

- **The narrative *then*** — what contemporaries believed an event meant as they
  lived it.

- **The narrative *now*** — the inherited, popular, or textbook version we hold
  today.

- **The gap** — the distance between the narrative then, the narrative now, and
  the underlying reality. A primary object of study.

- **Thread** — a live, unfinished force that crosses a year boundary (a war, a
  technology, a sharpening contradiction, a debt coming due). Given a stable name
  so it can be tracked across years. See [`connections.md`](connections.md).

- **Carry-forward** — Section 0 of a year file: the threads received from the
  prior year.

- **Handoff** — the final section of a year file: the threads passed to the next.

- **The continuity contract** — the rule that a year's handoff must match the next
  year's carry-forward.

- **Causal layers** — *event* (actors, contingency), *conjunctural* (a decade's
  pressures), *structural* (slow variables). See
  [`methodology.md`](methodology.md) §3.

- **The through-line / to today** — the running connection from any year to the
  present-day United States; the project's organizing question.

- **Steelman** — restating an opposing argument at its strongest before rebutting
  it. Mandatory before any rebuttal.

- **Cui bono** — "who benefits?"; the Heretic's first question of any narrative.

- **Macro-arc** — the decade-level synthesis of which threads dominated and how
  the decade moved the U.S. toward its present.

- **The textbook test** — if a paragraph could enter a high-school textbook
  unchanged, it is too shallow.

- **Irreducible disagreement** — a clash the three minds could not resolve,
  preserved (not dissolved) in synthesis because the disagreement is itself
  revealing.

- **Fact / Interpretation / Speculation** — the three epistemic tiers every claim
  is sorted into. Speculation must be flagged; contested facts must be marked.
