# Debate Protocol — The Turn-by-Turn Structure of a Year

This is the choreography the three minds follow for each year. It maps directly
onto the sections of [`templates/YEAR_TEMPLATE.md`](../templates/YEAR_TEMPLATE.md).
The point of a fixed protocol is to guarantee that every year gets the *same kind
of pressure*, so the series is consistent and comparable across two centuries.

---

## Phase 0 — Carry-Forward (Cartographer leads)

Before debate, the Cartographer restates the **threads handed forward** from the
previous year (see that year's final section). These become Section 0 of the new
year file. If this is 1800, the carry-forward is the standing conditions of the
world in 1799 (the inheritance of revolution, empire, and slavery).

> **Continuity check:** Section 0 of year N must match the handoff of year N−1.

---

## Phase 1 — Establishing the Six Events (all three, factual)

The personas agree on the **3 U.S. + 3 world events** per
[`event-selection.md`](event-selection.md). For each event they record the
**factual core** (datable, sourced) — no interpretation yet. Disagreement about
*which* events to include is itself logged.

For each event, also capture both narratives at a first pass:
- **The narrative then** (Herald drafts)
- **The narrative now** (Herald drafts, Heretic flags distortions)

---

## Phase 2 — Opening Interpretations (one uninterrupted turn each)

Each persona gives its reading of the year, from its lens, without interruption:

1. **Herald** — the meaning, the belief, the story then and now.
2. **Heretic** — the omission, the interest, the gap between word and deed.
3. **Cartographer** — the forces beneath, and the connections among the six events.

No rebuttals yet. Each turn must engage all six events, not cherry-pick.

---

## Phase 3 — Cross-Examination (structured rebuttal)

Now they engage each other. **The steelman rule binds:** before rebutting, a
persona restates its target's argument at full strength, to the target's
satisfaction. Then it presses.

Recommended exchanges (at minimum):
- Heretic → Herald: "Whose story is that, and who paid for it?"
- Herald → Heretic: "Was the belief not also sincere and consequential?"
- Cartographer → both: "Would this have happened anyway? Show me the agency."
- Either → Cartographer: "Where is the human choice your map erased?"

---

## Phase 4 — Pressure on the Narrative (then vs. now)

The whole table turns to the central project question: **the distance between the
narrative then, the narrative now, and the reality.** Where has the story been
sanded smooth? What does the present-day version let Americans avoid? What did
contemporaries see clearly that we've forgotten — or vice versa?

This is where the entry earns the word "philosophical." Spend here.

---

## Phase 5 — The Thread to Today (Cartographer leads, all contribute)

Aim every lens forward. How did this year contribute to where the United States
*actually is* now — its institutions, fault lines, self-image, and global
position? Name the specific present-day inheritance, not a vague "it shaped
America."

---

## Phase 6 — Synthesis (all three)

Record, honestly:
- **Convergence** — what all three came to agree on.
- **Irreducible disagreement** — what they could not resolve, *and why the
  disagreement is itself illuminating* (contested evidence? clashing values? a
  live political fight today?).

Do **not** manufacture consensus. A forced verdict is a failure condition
([`methodology.md`](methodology.md) §8).

---

## Phase 7 — Handoff (Cartographer leads)

State the **threads this year hands to the next** — live tensions, unfinished
wars, spreading technologies, sharpening contradictions, debts coming due. These
must be specific enough that next year's Phase 0 can pick them up verbatim.

---

## Quality gate

Before marking the year **done**, run it against
[`depth-rubric.md`](depth-rubric.md). If it fails any non-negotiable, it is not
done.
