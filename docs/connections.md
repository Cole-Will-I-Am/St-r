# Connections — The Threads That Make a River

History in this project is **one continuous river**, not 226 snapshots. Two kinds
of connection enforce that, and both are required in every year.

---

## 1. Intra-year connections (within a single year)

How the six events of one year relate to each other. Three sub-types:

- **U.S. ↔ World** — how a domestic event and a foreign one are linked (a war
  abroad reshapes a domestic economy; an invention here changes a market there).
- **Event ↔ Event** — direct causal or thematic ties among the six.
- **Invisible / structural** — events that *seem* unrelated but share a hidden
  driver (the same financial panic, the same technology, the same migration, the
  same ideology moving underground).

The Cartographer leads this, but all three contribute. **Beware connection
inflation** — only claim a link the evidence supports; coincidence is not
causation.

---

## 2. Inter-year connections (the handoff)

This is the spine of the whole project. Each year:

- **receives** a set of **threads** carried forward from the prior year (Section
  0 of the template), and
- **hands off** a set of threads to the next year (final section).

> **The continuity contract:** the *handoff out* of year N must equal the
> *carry-forward into* year N+1. If they don't match, one of the two years is
> wrong.

### What is a "thread"?

A live, unfinished force that crosses the year boundary. Examples of thread types:

- a **war or conflict** not yet resolved
- a **technology** spreading or maturing
- a **contradiction sharpening** (e.g., a free nation expanding slavery)
- a **debt coming due** — economic, political, or moral
- an **institution forming, mutating, or decaying**
- a **demographic wave** in motion (migration, urbanization, generational shift)
- a **narrative being built or breaking down**

Give each thread a short stable name so it can be tracked across years (e.g.,
`thread: the slavery-expansion contradiction`, `thread: the railroad/credit
nexus`). Reusing names across years lets the river be followed for decades.

---

## 3. The decade macro-arc

At the decade level (each decade README), the Cartographer synthesizes the
**macro-arc**: which threads dominated the ten years, which were resolved, which
were born, and how the decade moved the United States closer to its present shape.
This is the zoomed-out view that individual years can't see.

---

## 4. The destination

All threads ultimately point at one question:
**how did we get to where the United States is today?**

A thread that never, across its life, connects to the present is either mislabeled
or was never worth tracking. The through-line is the reason the river is being
mapped at all.
