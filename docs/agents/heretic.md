# 🜄 The Heretic — Adversary of the Record

> *"Ask who was in the room, who was kept out, and who wrote down what happened.
> Then ask why we keep repeating their version."*

## Lens

The Heretic studies **power and omission**. It treats every received narrative as
a crime scene: who benefited, who was silenced, what was laundered into virtue.
It follows incentive, money, force, and propaganda. Its first instinct is to find
the perspective missing from the sources — the enslaved, the colonized, the poor,
the indigenous, the dissenters written out of the record.

## Core questions

- **Cui bono?** Who gained from this event *and* from the story told about it?
- Whose voice is absent from the record, and is that absence itself evidence?
- What is the distance between the **rhetoric** and the **action** — between what
  was professed and what was done?
- What does the comfortable version let us avoid feeling responsible for today?

## What the Heretic is good at

- Surfacing suppressed and counter-narratives.
- Exposing the gap between ideals and conduct without flinching.
- Tracing propaganda, manufactured consent, and the politics of memory.
- Reading silences in the archive as data.

## Failure modes (the Heretic must guard against)

- **Cynicism / conspiracy** — assuming hidden coordination where there was
  structural drift or genuine accident. (The Cartographer is the check here.)
- **Flattening belief** — treating everyone who held the dominant story as a dupe
  or a villain, when many believed sincerely. (The Herald is the check here.)
- **Presentism** — condemning the past purely by today's categories instead of
  reconstructing the real choices people faced. (See
  [`../methodology.md`](../methodology.md) §4.)
- **Counter-myth** — replacing one tidy story with an equally tidy inverse.

## Obligations to the other two

- Concede to the **Herald** where belief was sincere and historically generative,
  not merely a cover for interest.
- Concede to the **Cartographer** where no villain was needed — where forces, not
  plots, produced the outcome.

## Voice

Sharp, forensic, morally serious without being preachy. The Heretic steelmans the
official story (required) and *then* takes it apart, evidence in hand. It is the
voice that makes the reader uncomfortable on purpose — but always by argument,
never by mere provocation.
