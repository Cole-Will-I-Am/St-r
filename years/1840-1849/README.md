## Macro-Arc of the Decade

- **Dominant threads:** The decade is defined by the collision of **demographic-fait-accompli** and **industrial-fracture**, where the sheer velocity of population movement (Oregon, Texas, California) and capital accumulation outpaced the static capacity of existing legal and political institutions to contain them. The "machine" of the 1840 election proved that mass mobilization could be engineered, but the subsequent years revealed that this engine could not be steered once ignited by the twin fuels of Manifest Destiny and industrial logistics. Every attempt to manage expansion through "gentlemanly" diplomacy or legislative compromise (Tyler's vetoes, the Oregon treaty, the Wilmot Proviso) was overwhelmed by the physical reality of settlers arriving before laws were written and gold being dug before treaties were ratified. The central force is the **sectional-velocity**: the rate at which new territory was acquired exceeded the rate at which the Union could negotiate its labor status, turning geography into an immediate existential threat to the federal structure.
- **Threads resolved:** The thread of **imperial-overstretch** in the British sense was resolved by the **british-retrenchment**; the disaster in Kabul and the repeal of the Corn Laws forced London to abandon direct territorial conquest in favor of informal empire and free trade, ceding the North American continent to the United States by default. Similarly, the **unequal-treaty-system** in East Asia hardened from a tentative experiment into a permanent structural feature of global commerce with the Treaty of Nanking, settling the question of whether the Qing could resist Western industrial coercion (they could not). Domestically, the ambiguity of **succession-norm** was stress-tested by Harrison's death and Tyler's accession; while controversial, the precedent that the Vice President becomes the President was functionally settled, removing a potential constitutional crisis from future calculations. The **rio-grande-flashpoint** resolved itself militarily by 1848, transforming a disputed border into a fixed, albeit resented, international boundary.
- **Threads born:** This decade birthed **congressional-paralysis**, a structural inability of the legislature to act on major moral questions without fracturing along sectional lines, first visible in the gag rule debates and crystallizing around the Wilmot Proviso. It generated **famine-demographic-surge**, a new variable in American politics where external humanitarian catastrophes (Ireland, Germany) instantly became domestic labor and electoral shocks, decoupling population growth from natural increase. The **gold-rush-prelude** evolved into **california-fait-accompli**, creating a precedent where remote territories could achieve economic critical mass and demand statehood outside the normal colonial gestation period, bypassing territorial governance entirely. Finally, **executive-war-power** emerged as a distinct thread, with Polk's manipulation of the Mexican War establishing a template for presidential initiation of conflict based on manufactured emergencies, independent of explicit congressional declaration.

## Carry-in / Carry-out

- **Inherited from the 1830s:** Entering the 1840s, the United States carried the **fugitive-diplomacy** tension, where the institution of slavery had become a friction point in foreign relations (the *Creole* case, British abolitionism) that the young republic could no longer ignore. The **industrial-fracture** was already present but latent, a growing divergence in modes of production between North and South that had yet to find a geographic flashpoint. The **demographic-pressure-valve** of the frontier was still viewed as a safety mechanism for social unrest, a belief inherited from the Jeffersonian era that land could solve class conflict. Lastly, the **administrative-hardening** of the 1830s left a legacy of party machines that were potent enough to win elections but too rigid to adapt to the centrifugal forces of rapid expansion.
- **Handed to the 1850s:** The decade hands forward **congressional-paralysis** as the primary governing reality for the 1850s, ensuring that any attempt to legislate on slavery will result in institutional gridlock or violent breakdown. It passes **california-fait-accompli**, a fully formed, populous, and wealthy state that forces the slavery question to a head immediately, destroying the possibility of gradual compromise. The **european-radical-exile** thread carries the ideological debris of the failed 1848 revolutions into American soil, importing sophisticated theories of class and rights that will radicalize domestic dissent. Most critically, **sectional-velocity** is handed off as an accelerating vector: the map is now full, the buffer zones are gone, and the next step in expansion must occur either by breaking the Union or by force of arms within it. The **class-warfare-memory** of the Astor Place Riot and the famine tensions provides the social tinder for the urban violence of the coming decade.
