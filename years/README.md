# The Years — Index & Through-Line

Every year from **1800 to the present** lives here, grouped into decade folders.
Each year is one markdown file built from
[`../templates/YEAR_TEMPLATE.md`](../templates/YEAR_TEMPLATE.md): six events
(3 U.S. + 3 world), the debate of the three minds, a synthesis, and a handoff to
the next year.

> **Read the river, not the puddles.** Each year *receives* threads from the last
> and *hands off* threads to the next (the continuity contract). Follow a single
> thread name across decades to watch a force travel two centuries to the present.

## How to navigate

- **By decade** — open a folder below; its README lists the years and the
  decade's macro-arc.
- **By thread** — pick a stable thread name and follow it forward year to year.
- **By question** — every year aims, somewhere, at the same target: *how did we
  get to where the United States is today?*
