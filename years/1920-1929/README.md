## Macro-Arc of the Decade

- **Dominant threads:** The decade is defined by the triumph of `closed-border-regime` and `managed-voluntarism`, a dual strategy where the state withdrew from moral guardianship but aggressively expanded its capacity to engineer social and economic containment. The primary force was the calcification of emergency wartime measures into permanent peacetime architecture: borders were not just patrolled but legally sealed against specific races; capital flows were not just monitored but looped through the `dawes-capital-loop` to create a fragile, debt-fueled prosperity that masked underlying stagnation. This era saw the rise of `aerial-policing-doctrine` and `broadcast-presidency`, technologies that allowed power to project itself over vast distances without the friction of local engagement, creating a "high-modernist" confidence that complex systems could be optimized by experts while dissent was marginalized as irrational or un-American. The structural logic was one of sterilization: sterilizing the gene pool via eugenics, sterilizing the airwaves via licensing, and sterilizing the global economy via gold-standard orthodoxy, all while ignoring the rotting foundations beneath the gold leaf.
- **Threads resolved:** The immediate post-war chaos of 1917–1920 was definitively resolved, but through suppression rather than reconciliation. The `red-scare-feedback` loop successfully contained the revolutionary impulse in the West, forcing the Soviet Union into the inward-looking `soviet-bureaucratization` that abandoned global revolution for "socialism in one country." The `imperial-fracture` of the Ottoman and Austro-Hungarian empires was settled into the rigid mandate system, drawing lines on maps that ignored ethnic realities to serve colonial logistics. Domestically, the question of Reconstruction's unfinished business was answered with finality: `racial-caste-reinforcement` moved from extralegal violence to codified law and restrictive covenants, effectively ending any federal pretense of protecting Black citizenship for another forty years. The `unfinished-armistice` in Europe was papered over by Locarno and Dawes, creating a false peace that resolved the immediate threat of French invasion but locked in the German resentment that would explode later.
- **Threads born:** This decade birthed the infrastructure of modern American soft power and internal control. The `cultural-epistemic-split` emerged as a permanent fault line, where scientific modernity and religious traditionalism ceased to be compatible worldviews and became opposing political identities, institutionalized by the Scopes trial and the rise of radio evangelism. `sun-belt-speculation` began here, driven by the automobile and air conditioning, shifting demographic gravity away from the industrial North toward a finance-driven, leisure-oriented South and West that would dominate late-20th-century politics. The `legal-insurrection-playbook` was formalized, teaching reactionary forces how to use court orders and federal inaction to nullify constitutional rights. Most critically, `engineered-vulnerability` was born: the deliberate construction of critical systems (electric grids, flood controls, financial markets) so interconnected and optimized for efficiency that they became brittle, turning localized failures into catastrophic systemic collapses.
- **Movement toward today:** The 1920s constructed the operating system of the modern United States by replacing the fragmented, localist republic of the 19th century with a centralized, technocratic, and segregated nation-state. It established the precedent that the federal government's role is not to ensure equity but to manage stability through `managed-voluntarism`—coordinating corporate elites while abandoning the vulnerable—a philosophy that would resurface in every subsequent crisis from the New Deal to the 2008 bailout. The decade cemented the U.S. as a hemispheric hegemon that preached openness while practicing exclusion, embedding `closed-border-regime` into the national psyche as a prerequisite for security. It created the "consumer-citizen," whose political agency was increasingly mediated by `broadcast-presidency` and purchased through credit, decoupling economic participation from production. The racial geography of the modern American city—white suburbs, Black ghettos, policed boundaries—was laid down in concrete during these years, making segregation a spatial and economic reality that no amount of later legislation could easily undo.

## Carry-in / Carry-out

- **Inherited from the 1910s:** The decade inherited the `total-war-industrial` complex, a massive engine of production that lacked a peacetime purpose and threatened to stall without new markets or enemies. It carried forward the `imperial-cost-shifting` dynamic, where the victors of WWI attempted to offload the financial burden of the war onto the defeated via reparations, creating a destabilizing flow of capital that demanded management. The `unfinished-armistice` loomed large, a geopolitical debt that had not been paid in trust or territory, only in temporary ceasefires. Finally, the `wealth-destruction-feedback` from the agricultural depression of 1920–21 carried forward, signaling that the rural economy had already collapsed before the urban boom began, a warning sign that the prosperity of the Jazz Age was built on a shrinking foundation.
- **Handed to the 1930s:** The decade hands off a fully primed `dawes-capital-loop` that is moments away from snapping, having created a transatlantic pyramid scheme of debt that requires perpetual growth to survive. It passes the `high-modernist-hubris` to the 1930s, a dangerous belief among elites that markets and societies can be engineered without consequence, which will collide violently with reality in the Great Depression. The `financial-sovereignty` thread is now critical: the question of whether the state can control global capital flows or will be destroyed by them moves from theoretical to existential. `asian-containment-failure` emerges as a new, urgent thread, marking the point where Western diplomatic constraints failed to check Japanese expansion, setting the stage for Pacific conflict. Lastly, `soviet-bureaucratization` is handed over as a completed transformation; the USSR is no longer a revolutionary beacon but a hardened rival state, redefining the global ideological struggle for the coming century.
