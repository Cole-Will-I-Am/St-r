## Macro-Arc of the Decade

- **Dominant threads:** The decade is defined by the violent transition from administrative anticipation to total mobilization, where the state's capacity to count and manage flows (1910) was tested until it became the sole architect of reality (1918). The central force is the collapse of the buffer zone between domestic stability and global chaos; the `central-banking-operational` thread evolves from a vacuum into the engine of war finance, binding American prosperity inextricably to Allied victory and erasing neutrality as a structural option. Simultaneously, the `labor-suppression-open-shop` dynamic hardens into a feedback loop of radicalization and state violence, as the machinery built to manage industrial efficiency (`total-war-mobilization`) turns inward to crush dissent when that efficiency is threatened. The `mexican-instability` thread acts as the persistent southern pressure valve, forcing the U.S. to define its border sovereignty through intervention rather than diplomacy, while the `fire-suppression-doctrine` metastasizes from forest management into a broader cultural refusal to accommodate systemic friction, preferring catastrophic rupture over controlled burn.
- **Threads resolved:** The `central-banking-vacuum` is definitively closed by 1913, replaced by an operational Federal Reserve that permanently alters the rhythm of credit and crisis, ending the era of panic-driven ad-hoc solutions. The `income-tax-ratification` thread resolves the fiscal architecture of the Gilded Age, shifting the state's revenue base from tariffs to direct extraction and enabling the massive spending required for modern war. The illusion of `progressive-realignment` as a coherent political coalition dissolves by 1919, revealing itself to be a temporary convergence of interests that could not survive the stresses of total war and imperial overreach. The `global-narcotics-regime` solidifies into a rigid prohibitionist framework, closing the door on the loose regulatory norms of the late nineteenth century and establishing the legal precedent for the war on drugs. Finally, the `balkan-powder-keg` detonates, resolving the long tension of European alliance rigidity not through diplomacy but through the exhaustive expenditure of blood and capital that leaves Europe dependent on American power.
- **Threads born:** The `submarine-warfare-doctrine` emerges as a new variable that shrinks the oceanic moat, making geographic isolation a technological impossibility and forcing the U.S. to project power globally to secure trade lanes. The `pathogen-vector` thread is born from the collision of total mobilization and global connectivity, demonstrating how biological agents can exploit state-built infrastructure (camps, transport networks) to achieve what armies cannot. The `red-scare-feedback` loop originates in the paranoia of 1919, creating a permanent structural mechanism where labor unrest is automatically conflated with foreign subversion, justifying preemptive suppression. The `imperial-fracture` begins here as the Ottoman and Austro-Hungarian empires shatter, generating a vacuum of authority in the Middle East and Eastern Europe that the U.S. will be forced to navigate for a century. The `racial-caste-reinforcement` thread is forged in the crucible of the Great Migration and the Red Summer, codifying a new, urbanized form of segregation that replaces rural Jim Crow with northern de facto apartheid.
- **Movement toward today:** This decade moves the United States from a continental republic with global trade interests to an indispensable hegemon whose domestic stability relies on global order. It institutionalizes the "permanent emergency," normalizing the suspension of civil liberties (`unfinished-armistice`) and the fusion of corporate and state power (`total-war-industrial`) as standard operating procedures rather than temporary exceptions. The self-image shifts from a neutral arbiter to a moral crusader (`democratic-universalism`), a rhetorical stance that masks the hard realities of economic imperialism and creates a recurring disconnect between American ideals and actions. Structurally, the decade locks in a path dependence where financial centrality (`central-banking-operational`) makes disengagement from world affairs economically suicidal, setting the stage for the Cold War containment strategy. The fault lines of race and class are not healed but reconfigured; the suppression of 1919 plants the seeds for the civil rights struggles and labor battles of the mid-twentieth century, while the open-shop victory delays union density for decades.

## Carry-in / Carry-out

- **Inherited from the 1900s:** The decade inherits the `central-banking-vacuum`, a critical weakness in the financial spine that leaves the economy vulnerable to liquidity crises and unable to project power effectively. It carries forward the `labor-suppression-open-shop` tension, where the legal and extra-legal mechanisms to break strikes are established but not yet fully synchronized with federal policy. The `mexican-instability` thread enters as a lingering post-colonial wound, reflecting the limits of U.S. influence in its own hemisphere. The `fire-suppression-doctrine` arrives as an entrenched bureaucratic mindset that prioritizes control over adaptation. Finally, the `global-narcotics-regime` and `income-tax-ratification` threads enter as unfinished legislative projects, representing the growing pains of a state attempting to regulate both the body politic and the global flow of commodities.
- **Handed to the 1920s:** The primary handoff is `unfinished-armistice`, a diplomatic and psychological failure that ensures the peace of 1919 is merely a ceasefire, guaranteeing a return to conflict within a generation. We pass forward `total-war-industrial`, a permanent complex of state-corporate integration that will not demobilize but merely shift targets from Axis powers to Communist blocs. The `red-scare-feedback` loop is handed off as an active, self-sustaining mechanism for political purification that will define the 1920s and resurface in the 1950s. `Imperial-fracture` is transferred as a geopolitical burden, leaving the U.S. to manage the debris of collapsed empires without a clear colonial doctrine of its own. Finally, `racial-caste-reinforcement` and `democratic-universalism` are handed off as contradictory forces: a domestic reality of entrenched inequality clashing with an exported ideology of freedom, a tension that will drive the next century of American foreign and domestic policy.
